Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Ragnarok Online?

No. Ragnarok: The New World is a new open-world game released in 2026, set in Midgard. It is not a remake of Ragnarok Online (2002), but you will recognize the world and its spirit.

Is the game free to play?

Yes. The game is free to download and play on Steam, with optional in-game purchases.

Is it pay-to-win?

The game includes optional in-game purchases, and player opinions on how much they affect progression are mixed. Since it is free, the best way to decide is to try it yourself before spending anything.

Does the game play itself?

The game offers an auto mode and offline resource accumulation, which help when you have little time. You can still explore Midgard, fight and take on bosses with your party yourself.
