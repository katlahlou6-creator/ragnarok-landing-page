Frequently asked questions

What happens when I click “Play Free Now”?

You'll go to the game's Steam page to start the installation. The button does not launch the game directly.

Is installing the game enough to start playing?

There's one more step: launch the game and follow the account instructions to log in and begin your adventure.

Is this Ragnarok Online?

No. It's a new game set in the same world of Midgard, with an open world and modern features.

Is it pay-to-win?

You can level up, explore and join boss battles without paying. In-game purchases are optional, but players who spend may progress faster and rank higher.

Can I try different classes with the same character?

Yes. With Job Freedom, one character can switch between all 8 classes.

Can my character earn resources while I'm offline?

Yes. The game includes offline resource accumulation.

Can I play on my phone too?

Yes. Log in with the same Gravity account on mobile and your progress follows you.
