Frequently asked questions

What happens when I click “Download free on Steam”?

It opens the game's Steam page, where you install the game on your Windows PC. It does not launch the game directly: you install it first, then log in from the launcher.

Which platform is this download for?

Windows PC, through Steam. You need a Steam account, and a Gravity account that you create in the game launcher.

Is installing the game enough to start playing?

There's one more step: launch the game and follow the account instructions to log in and begin your adventure.

Is this Ragnarok Online?

No. It's a new game set in the same world of Midgard, with an open world and modern features.

Does the game play itself?

The game includes an auto mode, which you can choose to use for routine tasks. Exploring, building your class and boss fights with your guild are the heart of the game.

Is it pay-to-win?

You can level up, explore and join boss battles without paying. In-game purchases are optional, but players who spend may progress faster and rank higher.

Can I play at my own pace?

Yes. The game includes offline resource accumulation: your character keeps collecting resources while you're away.
